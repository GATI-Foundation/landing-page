About

Evidence for safer, more scalable skills mobility

GATI Foundation (Global Access to Talent from India) is a non-profit building the systems, partnerships and pathways that make global skills mobility simpler, safer and more scalable.

These tools are part of GATI's data and research work. They give governments, employers, training providers and funders a shared, sourced view of where demand sits abroad, where Indian workers already go, and what it takes to prepare them at home.

How to read the data
  • 01
    Every figure is sourcedEach tool lists its datasets, publishers and reference years on the page.
  • 02
    Snapshot or live, always labelledJob-posting data is a dated snapshot. The news tracker updates every weekday. Each tool says which it is.
  • 03
    International classificationsOccupations follow ISCO-08 (ILO) and employer sectors follow ISIC Rev.4 (UN), so results compare across countries.
  • 04
    Raw counts firstMaps and tables show counts as collected, with notes on gaps and estimates, rather than composite scores.
